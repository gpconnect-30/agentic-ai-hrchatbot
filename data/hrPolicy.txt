ABC Technologies - Human Resources Policy Manual

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Welcome to ABC Technologies. This document outlines the company's general Human Resources policies. All employees are expected to read and comply with these policies.

Working Hours
The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees are expected to complete 40 working hours per week.

Attendance
Employees must report to work on time. If an employee is unable to attend work due to illness or an emergency, they must notify their reporting manager before 9:00 AM whenever possible.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week with manager approval. Additional remote work arrangements require approval from both the department head and HR.

Probation Period
New employees will undergo a probation period of six months. During this period, performance will be evaluated regularly before confirmation of employment.

Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain professional behavior, respect colleagues, and comply with company policies. Harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate workplace behavior will not be tolerated.

Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year. Managers evaluate employees based on performance, collaboration, communication, and achievement of objectives.

Learning and Development
Employees are encouraged to participate in company-sponsored training programs. Subject to manager approval, the company may reimburse approved certification costs.

Travel Policy
Employees traveling for business purposes must obtain manager approval before booking travel. All travel expenses must be submitted within 15 days after returning from the trip.

IT Security
Employees must not share passwords or company credentials. Company laptops should be locked whenever left unattended. Confidential company information must not be stored on personal devices.

Leave Policy
Detailed leave rules, eligibility, leave types, and approval processes are available in the Leave Policy document.
