ABC Technologies - Leave Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This document defines the leave entitlements and procedures for all full-time employees.

Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave every calendar year.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave each year. Medical certificates may be required for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

Casual Leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave should be requested at least two working days in advance unless it is an emergency.

Maternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in accordance with company policy and applicable labor laws.

Paternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave.

Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 3 days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member.

Marriage Leave
Employees are eligible for 5 working days of paid marriage leave upon submission of supporting documentation.

Leave During Probation
Employees serving their probation period are eligible for sick leave but may take annual leave only after receiving manager approval.

Leave Carry Forward
Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the following calendar year. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused leave days.

Leave Encashment
Unused leave cannot be encashed unless specifically approved under company separation policies.

Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all company-declared public holidays. If employees are required to work on a public holiday, they will receive either compensatory leave or additional pay as determined by company policy.

Leave Application Process
All planned leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal. Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests based on business requirements.

Emergency Leave
Employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible in emergency situations. Formal leave requests must be submitted after returning to work.

Questions
Employees with questions regarding leave eligibility should contact the Human Resources department.
